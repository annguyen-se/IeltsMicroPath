Techniques for Spotting Paraphrase Traps in IELTS Reading

3-Step Rule for Defeating Keyword Traps:

  1. Avoid exact match traps: IELTS examiners frequently plant exact words from the question into distractor options.
  2. Identify Word Class Shifts: Verbs in questions typically transform into noun forms in passages (e.g., degrade $\to$ degradation).
  3. Verify Subject-Verb-Object logic: Check whether the stated subject actually performs the action or has been mismatched.
