Techniques for Spotting Paraphrase Traps in IELTS Reading

3-Step Rule for Defeating Keyword Traps:

  1. Avoid exact match traps: IELTS examiners frequently plant exact words from the question into distractor options.
  2. Identify Word Class Shifts: Verbs in questions typically transform into noun forms in passages (e.g., degrade $\to$ degradation).
  3. Verify Subject-Verb-Object logic: Check whether the stated subject actually performs the action or has been mismatched.

Micro check question (Passing requirement: ≥ 80%):

Passage: "The company accelerated the automation process."
Question: "Which statement reflects the author's meaning?"

Confusion between 'substantial' and 'substantiated'

"The committee found ______ evidence to proceed with charges."

Root Cause Analysis: You selected substantiated (participle adjective meaning 'proven with proof'). The required word was substantial (considerable in quantity or importance). Error stems from confusion between shared root suffixes.
[Tiếng Việt: Nhầm đuôi tính từ -al (số lượng lớn) với phân từ -ed (đã được chứng minh)].
CODE: ERR_SYN (Syntax Structure) Logged: 3 days ago

Third Conditional Inversion without 'If'

"______ the government acted sooner, the crisis might have been mitigated."

Root Cause Analysis: Third conditional inversion rule requires Had + S + V3/ed. Selecting If had the government caused an invalid word-order error.
[Tiếng Việt: Đảo ngữ điều kiện loại 3 bỏ If phải đảo Had lên trước chủ ngữ].
